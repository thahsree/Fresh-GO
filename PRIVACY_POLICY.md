# FreshGo - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Compliance:** IT Act (India), Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, FSSAI Guidelines

At **FreshGo** ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and trust above all else. This Privacy Policy describes how FreshGo collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the FreshGo Customer Mobile Application and related services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Mobile Phone Number & Name:** Collected during passwordless OTP login to verify identity, manage your customer account, and prevent unauthorized access.
- **Delivery Addresses:** Street name, house/flat number, landmark, area, and city provided by you to fulfill physical doorstep delivery.

### B. Device & Location Information
- **Precise & Approximate Location (GPS):** Collected with your explicit consent to:
  1. Determine 10km express delivery radius from the nearest FreshGo fulfillment hub.
  2. Route delivery partners accurately to your doorstep.
  3. Ensure accurate cold-chain delivery timing.
- **Device Identifiers:** Device model and operating system version for push notification delivery (e.g. order dispatched, arriving in 15 mins).

### C. Order & Transaction Data
- **Order History:** Purchased fresh seafood and meat items, quantities, cut preferences, and order timestamps.
- **Payment Method:** FreshGo primarily operates via **Cash on Delivery (COD)** and doorstep UPI payment to the delivery partner. We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank account passwords on our servers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:
1. **Order Processing & Fulfillment:** Preparing fresh cuts, packing in insulated cold-chain boxes, and dispatching riders.
2. **Real-time Live Order Tracking:** Updating you as your delivery partner arrives at your location.
3. **Customer Support:** Resolving order inquiries, freshness guarantees, and refunds.
4. **Security & Fraud Prevention:** Verifying legitimate customer mobile numbers via OTP.

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Your data is only shared with:
- **Delivery Partners:** Rider is provided your delivery address, landmark, and phone number solely for the purpose of completing your active delivery.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** Secure cloud hosting and database systems (e.g. AWS Lightsail) equipped with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

---

## 4. Data Security

All communications between the FreshGo mobile application and our backend servers are encrypted using **HTTPS / TLS 1.3**. Access to customer databases is restricted to authorized personnel under strict role-based access control.

---

## 5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:
- **View & Update:** View and update your saved delivery addresses and account profile directly within the app.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** You can delete saved addresses from your Profile at any time. To request full account and data deletion, email our Data Privacy Officer at **chthashreef22@gmail.com** or contact customer support within the app. Your data will be deleted within 30 business days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

FreshGo does not target or knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.
